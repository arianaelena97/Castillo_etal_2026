These data are for Final Revised paper from ACP titled "Developing tracer interrelationships to derive stratospheric age of
air from satellite observations of nitrous oxide."

We provide two products:

(1) Latitude-varying SF6-age:N2O from MIPAS:
   -- We provide MIPAS and ACE-FTS 5-degree resolved SF6-age and N2O in file: MIPAS_ACE_zm_N2O_AoA_alt_lat_5deg
   
    -- We provide 10-degree resolved N2O and AoA (weighted and unweighted) in file: MIPAS_AoA_N2O_alt_lat_10deg
    
    -- We provide 10-degree and smoothed SF6-age and N2O in file: MIPAS_AgeN2O_relationships

(2) MLS N2O-age time series from 2005-2012: Age_Timeseries
